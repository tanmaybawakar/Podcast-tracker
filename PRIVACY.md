# PodTrackio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

PodTrackio is developed by TANGENIX PRIVATE LIMITED. This policy explains how the macOS app handles information.

## Information you provide

- Account information, such as your email address, is used to authenticate your PodTrackio account.
- Podcast library data, learning progress, categories, collections, goals, schedules, and playback position may be stored in the app and synchronized through Supabase when you sign in.
- If you add a Groq API key, PodTrackio stores it in the macOS Keychain and sends transcript context and your prompts directly to Groq when you request AI summaries or chat responses.
- If you paste or import a transcript, the app processes that content only to provide the feature you requested.

## Third-party services

PodTrackio communicates with Supabase for authentication and optional cloud synchronization, YouTube for video playback and metadata, TranscriptAPI for requested transcript retrieval, and Groq for AI features you explicitly invoke. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Local data

App preferences, cached content, and locally imported transcript files remain on your Mac unless a feature explicitly synchronizes the associated podcast or learning data. PodTrackio does not sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers.

## Retention and deletion

You can remove podcasts and local app data from within PodTrackio. To request deletion of account data stored by the service, open an issue in the project support repository without posting private information, and the developer will provide a private support channel.

## Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the PodTrackio support page: https://github.com/tanmaybawakar/Podcast-tracker/issues
